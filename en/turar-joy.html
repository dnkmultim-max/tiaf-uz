New residence

A single 400-bed student residence is planned for the Academy's students on a public–private partnership basis. The public–private partnership conditions for student residences set by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 563 of 9 September 2021 apply.

During the transition period

Students transferring from the Tashkent Institute of Textile and Light Industry to the Academy are allowed to use the Institute's student residence in the established manner until the Academy's student residence is put into use.

Source: Resolution PQ-378, Annex 2, para. 32
