Fees and grants

Contract payment procedure, online payment, grants and benefits.

Sources of funding

The Academy's activities are funded from state budget funds, income from teaching students on a fee-paying contract basis, as well as grants and other lawful sources.

Source: Resolution PQ-378, para. 11.

Online payment and electronic contract

Electronic contracts for students, online payment of tuition under fee-paying contracts and the accounting of funds are handled through the single information system for state higher education institutions — billing.edu.uz. A separate profile will be opened for the Academy in this system (the resolution sets a deadline of November 2026).

Source: Resolution PQ-378, Annex 2, para. 12.
