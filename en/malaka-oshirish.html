Professional development

Vocational training and professional development courses for light industry employees and students.

Courses from September 2027. A centre for retraining and professional development of industry specialists will start operating at the Academy.

Source: Resolution PQ-378, Annex 2, para. 19

Programmes

Vocational training courses will be organised for light industry employees and students in the following areas:

  • modern technologies and digital solutions;
  • design and modelling;
  • pattern construction;
  • production technologies and management.

Courses are delivered under vocational training, retraining and professional development programmes based on the needs of enterprises and organisations.
