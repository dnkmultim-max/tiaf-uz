About the Academy

The Tashkent International Academy of Fashion is a state higher education institution that trains highly qualified specialists for fashion, design and the creative economy.

16.10.2026date of the resolution on establishmentPQ-378
2026/27year the teaching process beginsPQ-378, para. 3
Parisjoint education programme partnerPQ-378, para. 3

The Academy is a state higher education institution operating under the Light Industry Development Agency. It was established on the basis of the joint education programme of the Tashkent Institute of Textile and Light Industry with the Paris International Fashion Academy.

Source: Resolution PQ-378, para. 3
