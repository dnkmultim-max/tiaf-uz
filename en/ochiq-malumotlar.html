Open data

Aggregated indicators for the Academy: student numbers and teaching staff. Download in CSV and JSON formats.

Personal data are not made public. All open indicators on the site are given in aggregated form.

Data are taken from HEMIS in aggregated form. The site does not write to HEMIS; it only reads. Personal data (full name, passport, an individual by age) are not included in open data.

No data yet. The teaching process begins in the 2026/2027 academic year, and the connection to HEMIS is planned for November 2026. Until then, the datasets on the list are shown here as descriptions.
