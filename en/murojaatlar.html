How an appeal is handled

  1. Receipt and registration

    The appeal is given a number and forwarded to the responsible unit.

  2. Review

    The responsible officer reviews the appeal. Approaching deadlines are monitored.

  3. Reply

    The reply is given in the way the appeal was submitted and in the status tracker on the site. The main time limit is 3 working days.

For certain appeals (for example, those requiring additional documents or checks), time limits and the accountability procedure are set by the Academy's regulations To be confirmed

Personal data

Personal data in an appeal are used only to handle that appeal. Consent to processing is requested in every form.
