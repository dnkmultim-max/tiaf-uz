HEMIS and LMS

A single entry point to the learning platforms.

Students and teachers reach the platforms through a single login: sign in once and work in all systems.

Login is not available yet. The buttons will become active once the platforms (HEMIS, LMS, Unilibrary) are connected and the single sign-on system is launched. For now, descriptions and deadlines of the systems are given here.

Platforms

HEMIS

Higher education management information system

Timetable, grades, curriculum, records. The system will be launched for the Academy in November 2026 and all necessary data will be entered into it.

HEMIS Mobile

Mobile app

HEMIS features on your phone. Availability and download links To be confirmed

LMS

Learning resources platform

Course materials, assignments, online learning. Launch date To be confirmed

Unilibrary

Electronic library

Online access to educational and scientific literature. The library collection will be digitised and added to the platform in May 2027.

Source: Resolution PQ-378, Annex 2, paras. 12 and 14

How login works

  • With single sign-on (SSO), one account gives access to all platforms.
  • Staff and student data are taken from HEMIS. The site does not write to HEMIS; it only reads.
  • You are directed to your account depending on your role (student, teacher).
