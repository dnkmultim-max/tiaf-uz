Sources of funding

The Academy's activities are funded from state budget funds, income from teaching students on a fee-paying contract basis, grants, income from services, sponsorship donations and other sources not prohibited by law.

In 2026, budget expenditure is carried out within the funds allocated from the republican budget to the education sector. From 2027, it is provided for in the State Budget parameters on the basis of budget requests submitted in accordance with budget legislation.

Source: Resolution PQ-378, paras. 11 and 12

Reports have not been published yet. The charter, the 2026 budget estimate and the staff list are planned to be approved in November 2026, so the first reports will appear once the relevant period has ended.
