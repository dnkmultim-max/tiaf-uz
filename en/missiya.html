Mission and resolution

Why the Academy was established, what goals it serves and what will be done and when.

Goal

To build in Uzbekistan a modern, internationally competitive education system in fashion, design and the creative economy, to develop the national fashion school and integrate it into the global fashion industry, and to train highly qualified specialists for the fashion industry.

Source: Resolution PQ-378, para. 1

Resolution

On 16 October 2026, Resolution No. PQ-378 of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan “On the establishment of the Tashkent International Academy of Fashion” was adopted. The resolution has two annexes: the main areas of the Academy's activity and the programme of measures for organising it.

Open the resolution as a PDF (19 pages, 4.5 MB)

Main areas of activity

  • training, retraining and upgrading the skills of highly educated and academic and teaching staff for fields such as fashion, design and the creative economy;
  • designing luxury light industry products with high added value, creating new national brands and shaping the Uzbek fashion segment;
  • developing and introducing study programmes together with leading universities in France, Türkiye, the United Kingdom and Italy on the basis of joint education programmes, and carrying out research;
  • research on harmonising national cultural heritage with modern design and innovation, creating new products and services and commercialising them;
  • cooperation with leading foreign higher education and research organisations and academic mobility of students;
  • internships and professional development for teaching staff;
  • commercialising the results of education, science and innovation, digitalisation, and the phased introduction of curricula and programmes;
  • early identification of young people's interest in fashion and design, career guidance and developing creative potential;
  • developing high spiritual and moral qualities in students and improving the effectiveness of the educational process.

Source: Resolution PQ-378, Annex 1 (abridged)

Turdimov and the Director of the Light Industry Development Agency N.Q. Kholmurodov.
