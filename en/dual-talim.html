Dual education

Training staff to order for enterprises: part of the study time is spent at the Academy and part at the enterprise.

2027/28academic year of introductionPQ-378, para. 3 (g)(iii)
30%of the learning process (at least) takes place at the AcademyPQ-378, Annex 2, para. 8
4 sectorstextiles, garment making, knitwear, leather and footwearPQ-378, para. 3

From the 2027/2028 academic year, dual education will be introduced as a form of study aimed at targeted training of staff to order for enterprises (organisations). At least 30 percent of the learning process takes place at the Academy and the rest at the commissioning enterprise.

  3. Study at the Academy and at the enterprise

    The curriculum is split between the Academy and the enterprise. Final-year students complete their internships at organisations where they may be employed.

For enterprises

  • employers' proposals are widely used in organising the learning process;
  • internship programmes for students' professional placements are developed and improved together with staff commissioners;
  • By the 2027/2028 academic year the Agency will identify the enterprises and organisations commissioning dual education and help establish cooperation with them.

Source: Resolution PQ-378, Annex 2, para. 8; para. 5, sub-para. (b) of the resolution
