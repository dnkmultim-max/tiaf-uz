Joint programmes

Joint education programmes with foreign partners, diploma conditions and student exchange.

The Academy operates on the basis of a joint education programme with the Paris International Fashion Academy. All students enrolled in the programme continue their studies at the Academy and receive the Academy's diploma.

Source: Resolution PQ-378, para. 3, sub-paras. (b), (v)

Double degree and joint degree

The aim is to reach agreements on training staff under joint programmes (double degree, joint degree) with leading universities in France, Italy, the United Kingdom and Türkiye.

Source: Resolution PQ-378, Annex 2, para. 23 (May 2027)
