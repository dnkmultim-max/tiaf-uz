Master's

Training in modern master's specialisations will begin in the 2027/2028 academic year.

Admission from the 2027/2028 academic year. No master's admission is opened in the 2026/2027 academic year. The programmes will be announced after passing state accreditation in the established procedure.

Source: Resolution PQ-378, para. 4; Annex 2, para. 5

Research activity

By 1 January 2027, the Academy of Sciences will set up the work of a scientific council at the Academy awarding academic degrees in the technology of textile, sewing and leather products. Industry specialists and scientists will be involved in supervising master's dissertations.
