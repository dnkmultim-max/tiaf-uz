For technical college graduates

Graduates of technical colleges who have successfully completed their studies and whose grade point average (GPA) is not lower than the threshold set by the Academy can continue their studies from the third year of the bachelor's programme on a fee-paying contract basis (from the 2027/2028 academic year). More details: Admission procedure.
