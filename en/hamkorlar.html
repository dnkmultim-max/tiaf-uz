The Paris International Fashion Academy and other foreign partners, joint programmes.

Paris International Fashion Academy

The Academy was established on the basis of the joint education programme of the Tashkent Institute of Textile and Light Industry with the Paris International Fashion Academy (Académie internationale de coupe de Paris).

All students enrolled in the joint education programme continue their studies at the Academy, and graduates receive the Academy's diploma.

Source: Resolution PQ-378, para. 3, sub-paras. (b) and (v)
