International applicants

Application, visa, registration and contract guidance for international applicants.

The Academy builds on international cooperation and provides for admitting foreign citizens for study.

Visa and registration

The Ministry of Internal Affairs, in the established manner and upon the Academy's requests, ensures the issuance (extension) of multiple-entry visas to foreign students admitted to the Academy and their registration (extension) at their place of residence.

Source: Resolution PQ-378, para. 14.

International cooperation

The Academy plans to cooperate on joint programmes (double degree, joint degree) and student exchange with leading universities in France, Italy, the United Kingdom and Türkiye.

Source: Resolution PQ-378, Annex 2, para. 23.
