About the Academy

A national fashion school — at a world-class level

The Tashkent International Academy of Fashion is a state higher education institution established under Resolution No. PQ-378 of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan dated 16 October 2026 and operates under the Light Industry Development Agency.

The Academy operates on the basis of the joint education programme set up by the Tashkent Institute of Textile and Light Industry with the Paris International Fashion Academy. Graduates receive the Academy's diploma.
