Registrar's Office

Services for students on a “one-stop shop” basis: online requests, electronic queue and status tracking.

Services provided to students at the Academy are organised through the Registrar's Office on a “one-stop shop” basis.

Source: Resolution PQ-378, Annex 2, para. 13

How it works

  1. Choose a service

    Find the service you need in the service catalogue. Catalogue To be confirmed

  2. Submit an online request

    Fill in the form and upload a document if needed. Your details are pulled in automatically through the single login.

  3. Queue and status

    You will receive a request number. Track the status on the site and through notifications.

  4. Receive the result

    The result is issued electronically or at the Registrar's Office. The request is handled within 3 working days.
