For technical college graduates

Technical college graduates who have successfully completed their studies and whose grade point average (GPA) is not lower than the threshold set by the Academy are allowed to continue their studies from the third year of the bachelor's programme on a fee-paying contract basis. The procedure applies from the 2027/2028 academic year.

Frequently asked questions

When does admission start?

The teaching process begins in the 2026/2027 academic year. Exact dates will be announced once approved by the Admissions Committee.

How many places are available?

In the 2026/2027 academic year, 50 applicants will be admitted to the Design bachelor's programme.

How is the exam held?

Applicants are admitted through creative entrance exams organised by the Academy. For the format and schedule, see the creative exam page.

Can a technical college graduate enter the third year?

Yes, if the GPA is not lower than the set threshold and on a fee-paying contract basis. Details are in the section above.

Can foreign citizens apply?

Yes. The conditions and procedure are on the “International applicants” page.

I couldn't find an answer to my question. Where can I write?

To send an online appeal to management, use the Virtual Reception. A separate form for admissions enquiries will also be added To be confirmed
